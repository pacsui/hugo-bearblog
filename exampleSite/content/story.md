## Learn More

This is a minimal Instagram story-like viewer built into Hugo.

You can add:
- **Videos** (MP4, WebM)
- **Images** (WebP, JPG, PNG)
- **Captions** with text overlay
- **Interactive navigation** between stories

Perfect for portfolios, galleries, or any media-rich content!
